Section III, cross-cutting discipline

Financial governance

Agents spend money every time they call a model or a paid tool, and they can spend it quickly. Financial governance makes agent costs visible, puts each one on a named account, stops spending at a limit before it happens, and checks that each agent returns what it was expected to.

What financial governance covers

Cost attribution

Every cost is assigned to a named account and cost center. When a marketing team's agent spends, the spending reaches the marketing budget.

Detailed tracking

Costs are tagged by agent, by type of task, by project and by business objective, and each piece of work records what it cost.

Model mix

Costs are tracked across the model providers an organization uses, so that each step can go to the model that does it well enough for the least cost.

Ceilings are the easy part of financial governance. The view across everything is harder, especially when work passes along a chain of agents. Cost across a chain is covered under Network, and each agent's cost is set against its value by the value owner named in section I.

Control objectives

There are 8 control objectives here. Each one states what it asks for, the risk it addresses and how it is measured. It then says what each risk tier requires, what evidence proves it, and who may check that evidence. The Operating Model page explains the tiers.

FIN-01

Every cost on a named account

Attribute all agent spending to a named account and cost center, so that someone answers for every cost.

Risk addressed
Spending that nobody answers for
Key metric
Share of agent spend attributed to an account

What each tier requires (the tiers)

  1. Tier 1 Exploratory
    Experiments charge to the team's sandbox budget.
  2. Tier 2 Operational
    Every agent's spending lands on a named cost center.
  3. Tier 3 Critical
    Each piece of work records the account that pays for it.
  4. Tier 4 Strategic
    Attribution is reconciled with suppliers' invoices each month.
Evidence
Cost records by account, and the reconciliation with invoices.
Who may check it
Internal audit and an outside auditor.
FIN-02

Spending ceilings

Set a limit per task and per period for each agent, and stop work that would pass a limit before it spends.

Risk addressed
Runaway costs
Key metric
Work refused at a ceiling, and spend over budget

What each tier requires (the tiers)

  1. Tier 1 Exploratory
    The sandbox has a fixed budget that stops spending when it runs out.
  2. Tier 2 Operational
    Each agent has a period limit set by its owner.
  3. Tier 3 Critical
    Each agent has limits per task and per period, enforced outside the agent before money is spent.
  4. Tier 4 Strategic
    Limit changes need finance approval, and breaches are reported to the executive sponsor.
Evidence
The configured ceilings, the record of refused work and any changes to the limits.
Who may check it
Internal audit, an outside auditor, and a partner.
FIN-03

Cost per job

Record what each piece of work cost, and set a baseline cost per task for each agent, so that inefficiency can be seen.

Risk addressed
Hidden inefficiency
Key metric
Share of agents with a cost-per-task baseline

What each tier requires (the tiers)

  1. Tier 1 Exploratory
    The experiment reports its total cost.
  2. Tier 2 Operational
    Each agent reports cost per task.
  3. Tier 3 Critical
    Each piece of work records its cost, and the baseline is reviewed each quarter.
  4. Tier 4 Strategic
    Cost per task is compared with other ways of doing the work.
Evidence
Cost per job records and the baseline.
Who may check it
Internal audit.
FIN-04

Unusual spending detected

Detect unusual patterns of spending that may point to a fault or abuse, and alert the owner.

Risk addressed
Cost problems found too late
Key metric
Mean time to detect unusual spending

What each tier requires (the tiers)

  1. Tier 1 Exploratory
    The sandbox budget is checked each week.
  2. Tier 2 Operational
    Spending is compared with the previous period each week.
  3. Tier 3 Critical
    Unusual spending alerts the owner within hours.
  4. Tier 4 Strategic
    Unusual spending can pause the agent automatically until the owner reviews it.
Evidence
Alert settings and the record of alerts raised.
Who may check it
Internal audit.
FIN-05

Return on investment

Track each agent's actual return against what was projected when it was approved.

Risk addressed
Agents that destroy value
Key metric
Share of agents meeting their return target

What each tier requires (the tiers)

  1. Tier 1 Exploratory
    The experiment reports what it cost and what it showed.
  2. Tier 2 Operational
    Actual cost and benefit are reported at the review date.
  3. Tier 3 Critical
    The value owner compares the return with the projection each quarter.
  4. Tier 4 Strategic
    The comparison is reported to the investment committee.
Evidence
The projection, the actual figures and the value owner's review.
Who may check it
Internal audit and an outside auditor.
FIN-06

Financial reporting

Report agent costs accurately and on time, so that decisions rest on good figures.

Risk addressed
Poor decisions from bad figures
Key metric
Accuracy of reported agent costs

What each tier requires (the tiers)

  1. Tier 1 Exploratory
    No separate report is required for an experiment.
  2. Tier 2 Operational
    Costs are reported each month by agent.
  3. Tier 3 Critical
    Costs are reported by agent, by business objective and by account.
  4. Tier 4 Strategic
    Reports are reconciled with the general ledger and reviewed by finance.
Evidence
The monthly reports and their reconciliation.
Who may check it
Internal audit and an outside auditor.
FIN-07

Acting on cost findings

Act on findings that would cut cost, such as sending a step to a cheaper model that does it well enough.

Risk addressed
Inefficiency that persists
Key metric
Share of cost findings closed

What each tier requires (the tiers)

  1. Tier 1 Exploratory
    No requirement applies to an experiment.
  2. Tier 2 Operational
    Cost findings are recorded.
  3. Tier 3 Critical
    Each finding is acted on or declined with a reason within a set time.
  4. Tier 4 Strategic
    Savings are measured and reported.
Evidence
The cost findings log with actions and savings.
Who may check it
Internal audit.
FIN-08

Chargeback and showback

Allocate agent costs to the teams that use them accurately enough that teams accept the charges.

Risk addressed
Disputed or wrong allocations
Key metric
Allocation disputes

What each tier requires (the tiers)

  1. Tier 1 Exploratory
    No allocation is needed for an experiment.
  2. Tier 2 Operational
    Costs are shown to the teams that use the agent.
  3. Tier 3 Critical
    Costs are charged to the teams that use the agent, by their use.
  4. Tier 4 Strategic
    Charges between organizations follow the contract, and both sides' records reconcile.
Evidence
Allocation reports and the dispute log.
Who may check it
Internal audit and a partner.
